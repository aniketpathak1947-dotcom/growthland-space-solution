## Brand & Style

### Personality & Market Position
The design system projects sovereign institutional authority, monumental structural scale, and fiduciary precision. Designed for a Tier-1 Pan-India commercial and industrial real estate advisory firm handling multi-million square foot logistics parks, Grade-A enterprise workspaces, and capital market land transactions, the interface communicates permanence, architectural intelligence, and investment acumen. 

### Emotional Response
The interface must inspire unassailable trust, boardroom gravitas, and decisive executive action. Users experience an atmosphere of strategic clarity: zero visual frivolity, razor-sharp operational metrics, and authoritative physical presence akin to an architectural monograph.

### Design Movement & Aesthetic Language
A synthesis of **Architectural Precision** and **Modern Corporate Institutionalism**:
- Monolithic structural grid alignment reflecting engineered logistics layouts and commercial blueprints.
- Deliberate use of high-contrast typography: authoritative serif display headings paired with technical, high-density sans-serif data layers.
- Deep, resonant surfaces grounded in midnight slate and titanium steel, counterbalanced by precision-engineered golden amber accents symbolizing prime value assets and tier-one liquidity.
- Tactical restraint: decoration is strictly functional, relying on micro-borders, clean technical hairline dividers, and disciplined spatial rhythm.

## Colors

### Palette Philosophy
The color system operates on an institutional hierarchy grounded in deep maritime night, structural slate, titanium alloy, and executive gold bullion:

- **Primary (`#0B192C` - Deep Midnight Slate):** Anchor for institutional credibility, foundational headers, dark monolithic footers, and key operational anchors.
- **Secondary (`#1E3E62` - Structural Marine Navy):** Supporting architectural elements, active navigation surfaces, interactive tab states, and data visualization baselines.
- **Tertiary (`#D97706` - Executive Amber Gold):** Reserved exclusively for high-value indicators, prime transaction metrics, Grade-A status badges, key conversion targets, and focus states. Never used for long-form text or non-critical fills.
- **Neutral Core (`#0F172A` - Obsidian Charcoal):** Used for maximum legibility in primary typographic hierarchies, high-contrast borders, and high-spec structural dividers.

### Supporting Canvas & Background Tones
- **Canvas Base (`#FFFFFF`):** Crisp pure white, giving executive clarity and high-contrast readability.
- **Titanium Grey 50 (`#F8FAFC`):** Alternating table rows, technical background canvas, and input default fills.
- **Titanium Grey 100 (`#F1F5F9`):** Card baseline backgrounds, subtle structural section panels, and metric badge backing.
- **Titanium Grey 200 (`#E2E8F0`):** Hairline borders, architectural blueprint gridlines, and structural card separation.
- **Muted Steel (`#64748B`):** Secondary metadata, geographic subtexts, footers, and transaction dates.
- **Gold Accent Light (`#FEF3C7` / `#F59E0B`):** Soft contextual badge fills and active gold hover states.

### Contrast Governance
All body copy, data metrics, and labels achieve minimum WCAG AAA contrast against light backgrounds. Primary CTA elements featuring `#0B192C` utilize `#FFFFFF` typography, while `#D97706` amber highlights utilize `#0B192C` or pure `#FFFFFF` depending on the minimum 4.5:1 / 7:1 ratio requirement.

## Typography

### Typographic Synthesis
The typographic strategy balances academic institutional prestige with modern industrial operational efficiency:

- **Display & Large Headlines (`Playfair Display`):** Selected for high-level editorial impact, investment overviews, and executive statements. Its classical serif forms evoke trust, wealth governance, and established market presence.
- **Core Interface & Body (`Plus Jakarta Sans`):** Delivers clean geometry, pristine legibility in dense multi-paragraph transaction briefs, and contemporary warmth that mitigates excessive corporate rigidity.
- **Metrics, Micro-Labels & Financial Data (`Inter`):** Deployed with tabular figures enabled for real estate metrics (square footage, transaction volume, cap rates, warehouse clearance heights). Uses uppercase tracked spacing (`letter-spacing: 0.08em`) for status pills, asset categories, and cadastral codes.

### Hierarchy & Alignment Rules
- Headlines never wrap past three lines; hero titles must be paired with tracked `label-technical` category markers directly above.
- Numeric data in dashboards and property matrices must always be right-aligned with tabular numbers enabled (`font-feature-settings: 'tnum' on, 'lnum' on`).

## Layout & Spacing

### Layout Model
The layout adopts a **12-column rigid architectural grid** with a maximum container width of `1360px` for standard analytical dashboards and marketing portfolios, expanding to `1520px` for expansive transaction pipelines and GIS-enabled industrial asset matrices.

### Breakpoints & Fluid Adaptation
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4-column layout, `margin: 1rem`, `gutter: 1rem`. Dynamic metrics cards stack vertically; multi-stage pipeline tables convert to accordion-style spec summary lists.
- **Tablet (768px – 1023px):** 8-column layout, `margin: 2rem`, `gutter: 1.5rem`. Side-by-side technical specification filters with persistent asset summaries.
- **Desktop (1024px – 1439px):** 12-column layout, `margin: 3rem`, `gutter: 2rem`. Full split-screen advisory capabilities, persistent lateral filter consoles, and dual-axis financial matrices.
- **Ultra-Wide (≥ 1440px):** 12-column centered layout with structured whitespace borders and fixed-width executive command centers.

### Rhythm Principles
- Space follows an architectural 8px mathematical baseline (`0.5rem`, `1rem`, `1.5rem`, `2.5rem`, `4rem`, `6rem`).
- Monolithic section dividers use `space-3xl` vertical padding on desktop to create deliberate visual rests between complex industrial sectors (e.g., Cold Storage vs. Hyperscale Data Centers vs. Grade-A Tech Parks).

## Elevation & Depth

### Philosophy: Structural Planar Layering
The design system abandons exaggerated floating shadows in favor of **structural planar elevation** and **architectural hairline boundaries**. The physical metaphor is that of layered structural drawings, blueprint vellums, and precision-cut marble slabs.

### Surface Tiers
- **Tier 0 (Foundation Canvas):** Pure White (`#FFFFFF`) or Crisp Titanium Sub-base (`#F8FAFC`).
- **Tier 1 (Structural Containers & Cards):** `#FFFFFF` framed with a calibrated 1px border of `#E2E8F0`. 
- **Tier 2 (Elevated Command Drawers & Active Asset Cards):** `#FFFFFF` surface lifted with an ambient, deep navy-tinted shadow: `0 4px 20px -2px rgba(11, 25, 44, 0.06), 0 2px 6px -1px rgba(11, 25, 44, 0.04)`.
- **Tier 3 (Executive Overlays, Modal Suites, Deal Term Sheets):** `0 24px 48px -12px rgba(11, 25, 44, 0.16)`, bordered with a refined dual-line treatment (1px solid `#CBD5E1` outer boundary).
- **Tier 4 (Dark Institutional Panels):** `#0B192C` base canvas with subtle inset micro-borders (`border-top: 1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.12)`) and deep architectural falloff (`0 32px 64px -16px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.35)`).

### Atmospheric Elements
Glassmorphism is used exclusively for sticky executive headers and analytical map overlay controls: `background: rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.88)`, `backdrop-filter: blur(12px)`, and bottom border `1px solid rgba(226, 232, 240, 0.80)`.

## Shapes

### Structural Geometry
Industrial real estate advisory requires high geometric integrity. The roundedness is defined at **Level 1 (Soft)**:
- Core UI elements (buttons, inputs, status tags, table cells) adopt a disciplined `4px` (`0.25rem`) border radius, communicating structural steel rigidity.
- Standard content cards, asset portfolios, and authority matrices utilize `8px` (`0.5rem`) corner radiuses (`rounded-lg`).
- Executive modal dialogs and deal-room drawer containers use a maximum of `12px` (`0.75rem`) (`rounded-xl`).
- Fully rounded / pill shapes (`9999px`) are forbidden except for live status pulse indicators (e.g., active leasing indicators). Sharp geometric discipline must prevail across all operational interfaces.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Institutional Button:** `#0B192C` background, `#FFFFFF` text, `font-family: 'Inter'`, `font-weight: 600`, `font-size: 14px`, `height: 48px`, `padding: 0 24px`, `border-radius: 4px`. Hover state transitions to `#1E3E62` with a subtle `translateY(-1px)`.
- **Prime Asset / Transaction CTA:** `#D97706` background, `#0B192C` or `#FFFFFF` text (high contrast), 48px height, `letter-spacing: 0.02em`. Hover shifts to `#B45309`.
- **Secondary Architectural Outline:** Transparent background, `border: 1.5px solid #0B192C`, `#0B192C` text. Hover fills with `#F1F5F9`.
- **Ghost Executive Action:** Transparent background, `#1E3E62` text, with an underline indicator that expands on hover from 0% to 100% width.

### High-Spec Stat Badges & Metric Tiles
- Structural boxes with `background: #F8FAFC`, `border: 1px solid #E2E8F0`, `border-left: 3px solid #D97706` for flagship assets.
- Contains an uppercase `label-technical` tracking label (e.g., "TRANSACTION VALUE", "GROSS LEASABLE AREA") in `#64748B`, followed by large tabular figures in `label-numeric-lg` (`Inter`, 700) rendered in `#0B192C`.

### Authority Matrices & Comparative Grid Tables
- Used for Pan-India location comparisons (e.g., Chakan vs. Sriperumbudur vs. Bhiwandi logistics hubs).
- Table headers: `#F1F5F9` background, `#0B192C` text, uppercase `11px Inter`, `padding: 14px 16px`, bottom border `2px solid #CBD5E1`.
- Rows: Alternating rows with `#FFFFFF` and `#F8FAFC`. Active or pinned rows highlighted with `#FEF3C7` (amber wash) and 1px `#D97706` border outline.

### Interactive Tabbed Capabilities
- Segmented pill/tab controls enclosed in a `#F1F5F9` container with `padding: 4px`.
- Inactive tabs: `#64748B` text, transparent background.
- Active tab: `#0B192C` fill with crisp white text, or pure `#FFFFFF` card tab with `box-shadow: 0 2px 8px rgba(11, 25, 44, 0.08)` and a bottom indicator line of `#D97706` (2px).

### Structured Transaction Pipeline Cards
- Real estate cards featuring a fixed 16:10 architectural photograph ratio.
- Top-right floating chip: `label-technical` (e.g., "MUMBAI EXTENSION - GRADE A") in white on `#0B192C` (85% opacity with blur).
- Lower card body: Playfair Display property title, followed by a 3-column micro-spec strip (Clear Height, Power Load, Floor Strength) separated by vertical `1px solid #E2E8F0` rules.
- Card Footer: Direct CTA ("Request Due Diligence Memo") paired with an investment status badge.

### Input Fields & Search Bars
- Institutional specification search bar with integrated location and square-footage dropdowns.
- `height: 48px`, `background: #FFFFFF`, `border: 1.5px solid #CBD5E1`, `border-radius: 4px`, `font-size: 14px`, `color: #0B192C`.
- Focus state: `border-color: #1E3E62` accompanied by a crisp `box-shadow: 0 0 0 3px rgba(30, 62, 98, 0.15)`. Error states utilize `#DC2626`.

### Executive Contact & Due Diligence Suite
- High-touch contact block framed in `#0B192C` dark canvas with subtle architectural grid lines in `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.05)`.
- Features advisor credentials, direct contact channels, verified RERA registration numbers, and an NDA-protected mandate request form.
